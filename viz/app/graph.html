How to use this map

Each circle is either a Part of the Manual or a provision of the Act or the Regulations that something cites. Each line is a citation the pipeline extracted. A bigger circle holds more; a heavier line was cited by more chunks. A provision nothing cites and that cites nothing has no line to draw and is not here — use the reader to browse an instrument in full.

With a mouse

  • Drag the background to pan
  • Scroll to zoom where the cursor is
  • Hover a circle for its name and size
  • Click a circle to focus it: everything else dims and its neighbours are listed below the map
  • Drag a circle to pin it where you drop it; double-click it to let the layout have it back

On a touch screen

  • One finger pans, or drags a circle
  • Pinch to zoom
  • Tap a circle to focus it

From the keyboard

Tab to the map, then:

  • ← → ↑ ↓ pan
  • + − zoom · 0 fit the whole map back on screen
  • n p step to the next or previous node, Parts first, then provisions
  • Enter focus whichever node is nearest the centre
  • Esc clear the focus

You never have to touch the map at all: search for a node by name below, and every neighbour in the panel under it is a button that focuses that node in turn.

Sharing what you are looking at

Focusing a node writes it into the address bar, so the URL you are on is a link back to this node. Open in the reader takes a node to its passages or its text; every Part and provision in the reader has a link back to here.

Type at least two characters, then pick a suggestion to focus that node.

Edges

Practice → law is a chunk's own provisions; law → law is a provision's, the compiled Act and Regulations citing themselves; within the Manual is a chunk's internal_refs, one Part pointing at another. Unticking a kind removes those lines, and any provision left with no line to draw.

The Manual states the Registrar's practice under the Trade Marks Act 1995 (Cth). It is not the legislation and does not bind the Registrar's discretion.
